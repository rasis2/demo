Module 14 · Communication

Communication Skills

A good technician is also a good communicator.

👂

Active Listening

Listen attentively, don't interrupt, repeat back to confirm understanding.

🗣️

Clear Explanation

Explain in plain language — avoid technical jargon with customers.

✍️

Written Skills

Write tickets, emails & documentation clearly, concisely and accurately.

Module 14 · Conduct

Professional Conduct

Your behavior reflects your professionalism.

🧍

Appearance & Attitude

  • Neat & appropriate dress
  • Be punctual
  • Positive, patient, respectful
  • Honest about the limits of your knowledge
🤝

With Customers

  • Never blame the customer
  • Explain the process clearly
  • Get approval before doing work
  • Follow up — make sure it's completed well
Module 14 · Interaction

Customer Interaction

Techniques for getting accurate information from customers.

❓

Open-Ended Questions

  • "What happens when you open the file?"
  • "When did this problem start?"
  • Encourages a full explanation
✔️

Closed-Ended Questions

  • "Is the computer turned on?"
  • "Do you see an error?"
  • For specific yes/no confirmation
Use empathy & patience — panicked customers (e.g. about to submit a presentation) need calmness and step-by-step guidance.
Module 14 · Documentation

Work Order & Ticketing

The ticketing system is the official record of all support work.

📝

Create Ticket

Record the problem, customer, time & priority.

🔍

Update Progress

Log steps taken, tests run & findings.

✅

Close & Follow-up

Document the solution, confirm with the customer, close the ticket.

Complete documentation helps other technicians & prevents rework.
Module 14 · Safety

Workplace Safety

Physical safety while working as a technician.

⚡

Electrical Safety

Unplug power before opening. Check for damaged cables. Don't work wet.

🧤

ESD & Handling

Anti-static wrist strap, ESD mat, lift heavy items with proper technique.

🪑

Ergonomics

Proper posture, screen at eye level, regular breaks — avoid injury.

Module 14 · Ethics

Legal & Ethical Issues

Professional and legal responsibilities.

🔒

Privacy & Data

  • Respect customer privacy
  • Don't access files without permission
  • Comply with policies & regulations (GDPR, PDP Act)
©️

Licensing & Copyright

  • Respect software licenses
  • Don't install pirated software
  • Follow agreements & organizational policies
