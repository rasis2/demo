Module 13 · Threats

Security Threats

Types of malware you need to recognize.

🦠

Virus & Worm

A virus needs a host; a worm spreads on its own over the network.

🎭

Trojan

Disguises itself as legitimate software — grants backdoor access.

💰

Ransomware

Locks data & demands a ransom. Backup is the best defense.

👁️

Spyware & Keyloggers

Watch your activity & silently record keystrokes.

🕳️

Rootkit

Hides inside the system — hard to detect, full control.

🪝

Botnets

Infected devices are used for DDoS attacks & spam.

Module 13 · Social

Social Engineering

Manipulating people — not technology — to gain access.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails/sites steal credentials. Check the URL & sender.

🎭

Pretexting & Baiting

A fake story (pretext) or luring bait (USB, prizes).

🚪

Tailgating

Sneaking in behind someone else without authorization.

📵

Shoulder Surfing

Watching screens/keyboards to steal information.

Module 13 · Auth

Authentication Methods

Verify user identity — something you know, have, or are.

🔑

Something You Know

Passwords, PINs. Make them strong & unique — don't reuse.

💳

Something You Have

Smart card, token, phone for OTP.

🫵

Something You Are

Biometrics — fingerprint, face (Windows Hello).

Multifactor Authentication (MFA) — combining two or more methods — is far more secure.
Module 13 · Defense

Firewalls & NAT

Network-level defense.

🧱

Firewall

  • Filters traffic based on rules
  • Blocks unauthorized ports & addresses
  • Software (Windows Defender) & hardware
🔀

NAT

  • Hides internal IPs
  • Port forwarding for specific services
  • An extra layer of protection
Module 13 · Encryption

Encryption & VPN

Protect data — at rest and in transit.

🔒

Data at Rest

BitLocker (Windows), FileVault (macOS) — full-disk encryption.

🔐

Data in Transit

TLS/HTTPS for the web; SSH for remote access — data is encrypted.

🌐

VPN

A secure tunnel over the internet — remote access & privacy.

Module 13 · Wireless

Wireless Security

Protect your Wi-Fi network from intrusion.

🔐

WPA2 / WPA3

Modern encryption — use a strong password. Avoid WEP (insecure).

📶

SSID & MAC Filter

Hide the SSID & limit devices through MAC filtering.

👥

Guest Network

Separate guests from the main network — VLAN/AP isolation.

Module 13 · Data

Data Protection

Data is your most valuable asset — protect it from every angle.

💾

Backup

3-2-1: 3 copies, 2 media, 1 offsite.

🛡️

RAID

Disk redundancy — but not a substitute for backup!

🗑️

Secure Disposal

Securely wipe data / destroy drives before disposal.
