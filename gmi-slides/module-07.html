Module 07 · Laptops

Laptop Components

Laptops pack all components into a compact space — often using specialized parts.

⌨️

Built-in Peripherals

Keyboard, touchpad, screen, speaker, webcam — all integrated into the case.

🔋

Proprietary Parts

Motherboards, batteries & parts are often brand/model-specific — not standard.

💾

Upgradeable

RAM & storage (M.2/SATA) can usually be upgraded; the CPU typically stays.

Module 07 · Power

Power Management

Battery and power saving are essential for mobile users.

🔋

Battery Types

Li-ion / Li-Polymer — rechargeable, sensitive to extreme temperatures.

🔌

AC Adapter

Wattage must match the laptop — a wrong adapter can damage the battery.

⚙️

Power Settings

Power plans (Balanced/Power Saver), sleep/hibernate, battery saving.

Module 07 · Connectivity

Wireless Connectivity

How laptops & mobile devices connect.

📶

Wi-Fi

IEEE 802.11 (a/b/g/n/ac/ax) — cable-free network access.

🔷

Bluetooth

PAN — earbuds, mouse, short-range file transfer.

📡

WWAN / Cellular

4G/5G via SIM — internet anywhere.

Module 07 · Docking

Docking & Port Replicators

Expand laptop ports for an office environment.

🧳

Docking Station

  • Video, USB, Ethernet, audio ports
  • Often self-powered
  • Click the laptop in — full connection
🔁

Port Replicator

  • Replicates laptop ports only
  • Usually via USB-C / Thunderbolt
  • Compact & portable
Module 07 · Mobile Devices

Tablets & Smartphones

Mobile devices — from hardware to connectivity.

📱

Hardware

SoC (CPU+GPU), touchscreen, cameras, sensors (GPS, gyroscope), battery.

🔌

Connectivity

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, NFC (payments), USB-C.

💳

SIM & eSIM

Physical SIM or digital eSIM for cellular connectivity.

Module 07 · Mobile OS

Android & iOS

The two dominant mobile operating systems.

🤖

Android

  • Google — open, many manufacturers
  • Apps: Google Play Store
  • Customizable by manufacturers
🍎

iOS

  • Apple — closed, consistent
  • Apps: App Store
  • Updates controlled by Apple

Troubleshooting Mobile Devices

Common issues & how to fix them.

ProblemSolution
Battery drains fastCheck app usage, power settings
Wi-Fi won't connectRestart device & router, forget & reconnect

Wrap Up

Key Takeaways

💻

Portable Parts

Laptop parts are specialized — limited upgrades, repairs need expertise.

🔋

Power is King

Manage power & use the right adapter for long battery life.

📶

Stay Connected

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular — understand the connection modes.

🩺

Fix Smart

Restart & check apps/power first before replacing parts.
